Who We Are

uClean is a mobile application developed by the uClean Kim Team for the UCAC — ICAM university community, with particular attention to the needs of Cameroonian institutions.

Publisher: uClean Kim Team
Institution: ICAM
Contact email: support-it@icam.fr
Data Protection Officer (DPO): Pere Maximilien Bossou Constant

Our Commitment

We are committed to protecting your privacy while contributing to a healthier environment for our academic communities.

Core Principles

  • Full transparency in data collection and use
  • Informed consent required before any collection
  • Data minimization — only data necessary for our educational purposes
  • Enhanced security via AES-256 encryption and TLS/SSL transmission
  • Full user control over your data

Data We Collect

1. Account Data (Required)

DataPurpose
Full nameAccount identification, personalization
Email addressAccount creation, login, essential communications
User IDAccount management, backend synchronization
PasswordAuthentication (encrypted)

2. Approximate Location Data

  • Type: WiFi network names (SSID) you connect to
  • Precision: Approximate location only (area > 3 km²)
  • We do NOT collect your precise GPS location
  • Android Permission: ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION (required by Android to read WiFi name, but we do not use GPS)
  • Android Permission: ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION
  • Purpose: Automatic trip detection for carbon footprint calculation

3. Physical Activity Data

  • Type: Daily step count
  • Android Permission: ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION
  • Purpose: Distance estimation, eco-friendly gamification

4. Trip Data

  • Trip start and end times
  • Distance traveled (automatically calculated)
  • Personal/professional classification (via your direct choice)
  • Estimated means of transport
  • Purpose: Carbon footprint calculation, educational tracking

5. Educational and Gamification Data

  • Educational articles read
  • Reading progress
  • Gamification points earned
  • Achievements and badges
  • Purpose: Measuring educational engagement, encouraging eco-responsible behavior

6. Technical and Performance Data

  • Crash logs
  • App diagnostics (performance, latency)
  • Device identifiers (Device ID)
  • Purpose: App improvement, bug fixing

7. App Interactions

  • Pages visited, features used
  • Purpose: Anonymized analytics to improve user experience

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Your precise GPS location
  • Your phone contacts
  • Your calls or SMS/MMS
  • Your financial or payment data
  • Your health information
  • Your photos, videos, or audio files
  • Your calendar/agenda
  • List of apps installed on your device
  • Any discriminatory information (race, religion, sexual orientation, political opinions)
  • Any data unrelated to our environmental purpose

What We Do NOT Do

  • We do not sell any personal data to third parties
  • We do not use your data for advertising or marketing purposes
  • We do not transfer your personal data to commercial companies

Data Transfers

Your data is hosted on servers located in the European Economic Area (EEA) via the Xano platform. Appropriate safeguards (standard contractual clauses) govern any potential transfer outside the EEA.

Data Security

  • AES-256 encryption for all sensitive data stored locally
  • TLS/SSL (HTTPS) transmission for all backend communications
  • Local storage on your device via SharedPreferences
  • Optional synchronization — you remain in control
  • Limited access restricted to authorized teams only
  • Regular security audits

Your Rights

In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Cameroonian Law No. 2010/012, you have the following rights:

RightDescriptionHow to Exercise
Right of accessView all your dataVia app > Profile
Right to rectificationCorrect inaccurate dataVia app > Profile
Right to erasureDelete all your dataVia app > Delete account OR email support-it@icam.fr
Right to objectStop all data processingVia app > Settings
Right to portabilityExport your data (JSON/CSV)On request at support-it@icam.fr
Withdrawal of consentWithdraw consent at any timeVia app > Settings

To exercise your rights, contact us at support-it@icam.fr. We will respond within a maximum of 30 days.

If you believe your rights are not being respected, you may lodge a complaint with the CNIL (www.cnil.fr) or your country's data protection authority.

Account Deletion

In compliance with Google Play requirements, you can delete your account and all associated data:

  1. From the app: Go to Settings > Your Account > Delete Account
  2. By email: Send a request to support-it@icam.fr with the subject "uClean Account Deletion"
  3. Online: Visit https://ken-andre.github.io/uClean/data-deletion

Deletion is permanent and irreversible. We do not practice temporary retention or account "freezing." We confirm deletion within 30 days.

Children

The uClean application is intended for university students and staff. It is not intended for children under 16 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from minors under 16. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a minor, please contact us immediately.
