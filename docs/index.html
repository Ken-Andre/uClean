About uClean / A propos d'uClean

uClean is a mobile application that helps the university community measure and reduce its carbon footprint through daily mobility tracking. Designed for UCAC - ICAM students and faculty in Cameroon.

uClean est une application mobile qui aide la communaute universitaire a mesurer et reduire son empreinte carbone grace au suivi de la mobilite quotidienne. Concue pour les etudiants et le personnel de l'UCAC - ICAM au Cameroun.
